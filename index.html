Data Science · FeaturedOct 2026

RetainIQ: Customer Retention Analytics

  • Built a leakage-safe churn model on 779K retail transactions (5.3K customers) using SQL features and a 90-day time-based label — XGBoost reached 0.81 ROC-AUC, with SHAP showing recency as the main driver.
  • Segmented customers with RFM and K-Means; found that 16% of customers bring 67% of revenue.
  • Ranked customers by churn probability × expected value to flag £745K revenue at risk; a simulation (assumed 20% offer effect) showed targeting the top 500 saves ~4x more than random outreach.
  • Deployed an interactive Streamlit dashboard with filters, a downloadable call list, and a campaign simulator.
